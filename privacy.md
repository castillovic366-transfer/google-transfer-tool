# Privacy Policy

This tool is designed for personal use to transfer data between two Google accounts owned by the same user.  
It does not store, share, or transmit any user data to third parties.

## Data Access
- Google Drive files
- Gmail messages
- Google Contacts
- Google Photos media

## Data Usage
Data is accessed only during transfer and is not stored beyond the session.  
OAuth tokens are saved locally (`.pickle`) and never uploaded or shared.

## Control
You may revoke access at any time via your Google Account security settings.
